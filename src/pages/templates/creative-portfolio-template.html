About

Who I Am

I'm a Los Angeles-based digital artist and creative director with a passion for creating visually stunning experiences. My work spans digital illustration, motion graphics, brand identity, and interactive media. I believe in pushing creative boundaries and creating work that makes people feel something.

Over the past 9 years, I've collaborated with brands like Nike, Spotify, and Netflix, helping them bring their visions to life through compelling visual storytelling. My approach combines artistic intuition with strategic thinking to deliver work that resonates with audiences.

When I'm not creating, you'll find me exploring new art forms, mentoring emerging artists, or discovering inspiration in unexpected places.
