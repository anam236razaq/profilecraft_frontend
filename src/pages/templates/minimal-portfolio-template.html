About Me

I'm a product designer based in Copenhagen with a passion for creating digital experiences that make people's lives easier. I believe that great design is invisible—it should feel natural and intuitive, never forcing users to think too hard.

Over the past 7 years, I've worked with startups and established companies alike, helping them transform complex problems into elegant solutions. My approach combines user research, iterative design, and close collaboration with engineering teams.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me cycling through the city, exploring Scandinavian design, or experimenting with new creative tools.
